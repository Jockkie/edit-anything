# Privacy

Video carries faces, voices, homes, screens and other people. Treat all of it as personal data.

- **Stays local by default.** Footage, takes, voices and transcripts are processed on the user's machine. Nothing is uploaded to an external tool without explicit consent for that file and that tool.
- **Other people in the frame.** Before using footage with other recognisable people (clients, family, people in public), ask whether they agreed. Blur or crop when in doubt.
- **Children.** Never generate, alter or publish footage of minors beyond what the user explicitly asks for their own family use; never send it to an external tool.
- **Voices.** A cloned voice only of a speaker who consented, and only for the purpose they agreed to.
- **Screens.** Check screen recordings for names, emails, messages, keys and notifications before they go into the film. Cover them.
- **Transcripts and dossiers** are files the user keeps. Do not keep copies outside the project folder.
- **Reports and examples** (for issues or evals) are anonymised: no names, faces, voices or client data.
