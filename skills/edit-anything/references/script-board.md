# The script board

The board is the plan the whole film is built from. One row per line of voice. Three columns that belong together, and a few that make it buildable:

| # | Said (the words) | Seen (what is on screen) | Lands on (the word) | Layout | Engine | Shot type | Length (s) | Cost |
|---|------------------|--------------------------|---------------------|--------|--------|-----------|-----------|------|
| 1 | "Most people never finish a video." | Empty timeline, one clip falls off | "never" | full frame | Explainer Studio | code | 2.1 | 0 |

The template is `templates/script-board.csv`. Every row needs said, seen and lands on before Gate 2.

## Writing what is said (Copywrite Me's method, built in)

- **Hook in the first 1.5 to 2 seconds.** Show the payoff first, then the setup. The first frame must make sense with the sound off.
- **One idea per video.** If there are two, there are two videos.
- **The viewer's words.** Use phrases from the user's customers, comments or own speech; avoid jargon.
- **One ask at the end**, the action from the goal (`references/interview.md`).
- **Claims:** only what the user can prove. Mark every number or promise in a claim register (claim, proof, source); unproven claims are cut or softened.
- **Write for the ear:** short sentences, numbers as they are said, names spelled as pronounced. Capitals only on screen.
- **Size it to the pace:** a cloned voice about 2.2 to 2.4 words a second including pauses, a person on camera about 2.6. 30 seconds holds about 65 to 75 words.

## Planning what is seen

- Every line gets one picture idea. The picture shows what the words cannot: the thing named, the number, the before and after, the process.
- **Lands on:** the one word in the line where the picture changes. Titles land on their word; a demo cuts on the line that names it; an effect hits on the reveal.
- **Layouts for a talking head:** full face (the ask, the personal line), split (face and picture), bubble (face small over a picture), no face (a pure B-roll or animation moment). Let the face grow as the ask grows.
- **B-roll:** the user's own footage first, then code-made scenes, then generated shots (with a quote), then licensed stock. Never footage of real people who did not agree.
- Mark each row's **shot type**: `code` (drawn by an engine), `footage` (the user's), `generated` (Flora, with cost), `screen` (a screen recording).

## From an existing take (route C)

Build the board from the transcript instead: each kept sentence is a row; "said" is the transcript text with its word times; plan "seen" for each kept row. Cuts are marked in the transcript (strike-through or `[cut]`), never by deleting words from the record.

## Gate 2

Show the board as a table. Ask the user to read it top to bottom and react; change rows they mark. Gate 2 passes when they approve the whole board and every paid row has an approved quote.
