# video-shotcraft

**What it is.** A skill that makes product and launch promos from the user's real pages: it captures screenshots, picks shots from a library of tuned motion recipes (124 cards, 150 shots), storyboards, animates in Remotion, cuts to the beat, adds sound design, checks every frame and renders an MP4. Apache 2.0. A narration sibling, video-talkcraft, locks every motion beat to a voiceover.

**The method.** Real screenshots and copy, never generated UI. Shot recipes as cards. Beat sync and frame QA built in. Exports to a browser editor (Motion Workbench) and to an editable JianYing (CapCut China) draft with clips, captions and separate effect and music tracks.

**Use it for.** Product launches, feature promos, SaaS and app demos (route A or B).

**Needs.** Node, Remotion, Chrome; the user's product pages or screenshots.

**Where it fails.** Anything without a product UI; talking heads (use route C).
