# pixelfx

**What it is.** 244 pixel-art effects and animations (fire, explosions, magic, weather, UI, pickups, transitions, critters), each a small piece of code, plus a skill that makes new ones "the way a picky pixel artist would": draft, render, look, refine. MIT (Patch). The method credits Hugo Duprez.

**The method.** Each effect is a file that draws into a pixel frame at a chosen resolution; effects are deterministic with a seed, can be recoloured with a palette and mirrored, and export to sprite sheets.

**Use it for.** Game-like, playful or retro moments: a coin burst on a sale, a level-up on a milestone, a pixel transition. Pair with Explainer Studio's Retro Digital looks.

**Make it the user's.** Recolour every effect with the `Look.md` palette.
